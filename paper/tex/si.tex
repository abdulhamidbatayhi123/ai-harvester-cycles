\section*{Supporting Note~\snote{1}: The learned models and their harness record}

\paragraph{Models.} The gray box L1 multiplies the rate of B0 by a bounded positive factor,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\mathrm{d}w}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{\kpp}{m}\,\bigl(\RHf - \RHb(w)\bigr)\,\exp\!\bigl(2\tanh g_\theta(z)\bigr),
\end{equation}
so the rate is still zero at equilibrium and uptake cannot move against the driving force. The input $z$ holds the
uptake normalized by the isotherm's uptake at \V{si_w30}{30}\,\% RH, $\RHf$, $\RHb$, the local isotherm slope, the
branch flag, the temperature, $\C{1}/m$, and the driving force $\RHf - \RHb$ itself (added after round~\ID{6}, below). The
black box L2 is a neural ODE, $\mathrm{d}w/\mathrm{d}t = (S/m)\,f_\theta(z)$ with a fixed unit scale $S$, given the
same local isotherm features and no physics: no sign constraint and no zero rate at equilibrium. Both are small
multilayer perceptrons trained in two stages, first on finite-difference rates (stage A) and then on open-loop
rollouts over short windows (stage B), and validated and early-stopped on the median held-out error $E$.

\paragraph{Harness.} Synthetic trajectories were generated on the real forcing, isotherms and dry masses from a known
truth and run through the fold pipeline. In world N the truth is B0 with the pooled $\kpp$ of the real data. The
correction worlds multiply B0's rate by a known factor that a model given only the isotherm can learn:
\begin{align}
\text{C:}\quad & \exp(-0.7\,w/w_{30}), \\
\text{C2:}\quad & \exp\bigl(0.8\tanh(d/15)\bigr), \quad d = \RHf - \RHb(w)\ \text{in \% RH}, \\
\text{C3:}\quad & \exp\bigl(-0.6\tanh(4(\RHb/100 - 0.25))\bigr).
\end{align}
The harness passed if (\C{1}) in world N, B0 refitted on the training sorbents recovers $\kpp$ within
\V{si_kerr}{2}\,\%; (\C{2}) in a correction world not used to develop L1, L1 beats B0 by the decision rule of the main
text on every held-out sorbent and halves B0's median $E$; and (\C{3}) in world N, no learned model beats B0. A judging
world, once used for diagnosis, could no longer judge: the judging world moved from C (rounds \ID{2} to \ID{5}) to C2
(round~\ID{6}) to C3 (round~\ID{7}), and C3 was defined and committed before any run on it. A null study (criterion
\C{4}) was designed to bound false beats of a learned model before any positive verdict; it was not run for L1 after
round~\ID{7}, because there was no positive result for it to protect.

\paragraph{Record.} Table~\ref{tab:harness} lists every case. In round~\ID{1} L1 missed the injected correction and L2 failed even
on correction-free data. In round~\ID{2}, with two-stage training, L1 found the correction, but L2 fitted the
training sorbents and not an unseen one: a global encoding of the isotherm let it memorize material identity, so it
was rebuilt on local isotherm features. In round~\ID{3} the rebuilt L2 still did not transfer, because its output was
scaled by a per-sorbent number the network never saw; its output became a water mass flux. In round~\ID{4} the
comparator was fitted by the scored metric and absorbed part of the injected slow-down, and L1 no longer beat it; L2
again failed world N and was dropped after a diagnostic showed that it could not hold the equilibrium precisely
enough over long plateaus. Diagnostics traced L1's bias to its stage-A target (a finite-difference rate paired with
the instantaneous rather than the window-averaged humidity); round~\ID{5} fixed it and L1 then failed on the
consistency of the gain over clusters, which led to weighting each segment on the scale of the scored relative error.
In round~\ID{6} L1 passed world C, by then a reported world only, and failed the fresh world C2; there the rule of the
time declared a beat on ROS-039 although L1's median error was larger than B0's, which led to condition~\C{4} of the
decision rule. The driving force was then added to L1's inputs. Before round~\ID{7} ran in world C3 a stopping rule
was written: L1 would not be redesigned again, and C3 would be judged on three held-out sorbents, adding Al-fumarate,
which had never been held out in any diagnostic.

\paragraph{Round~\ID{7} and the outcome.} Criteria \C{1} and \C{3} passed on all three sorbents. Criterion \C{2} failed on
MOF-303: in world C3, L1 lowered the median $E$ from \V{si_mof_b0}{0.0128} to \V{si_mof_l1}{0.0070}, but the median
cluster gain, \V{si_mof_gain}{0.0022}, was below $F = \V{si_mof_F}{0.003}$, the signed-rank $p$,
\V{si_mof_p}{0.034}, was above \V{si_alpha}{0.025}, and the ratio, \V{si_mof_ratio}{0.55}, missed the halving. On
ROS-039 and on Al-fumarate L1 met every condition (ratios \V{si_ros_ratio}{0.36} and \V{si_alfu_ratio}{0.42}). Under
the stopping rule no learned model passed the harness, so H4a is reported as not tested and no learned model was
fitted to the real data. The result concerns the method, not the data: a gray-box learner given only isotherms did not
reliably recover a known, injected, isotherm-dependent rate correction of this size at the noise floor of these
measurements. No statement is made about whether learning could help on the real data.

\section*{Supporting Note~\snote{2}: The B2 harness}

\paragraph{Design.} The harness of H4a$'$ (version~\ID{2}, committed before its first run; an earlier version was
revised after an independent review, and its only run was stopped before any result was read) uses the real forcing,
isotherms and dry masses of the primary set and the two composites, and the same autocorrelated noise as the harness
of Note~\snote{1}. Each world runs the \V{si2_nfold}{8} LOSO folds; in each fold B0 and B2 are fitted on the training
sorbents by the scored metric, and the decision rule of the main text is applied with each material's real noise
floor and the admissibility check. In world N the truth is B0 with the pooled $\kpp$ of the real data. In world K
(plumbing) the log rate constant of each sorbent is shifted by $\V{si2_bK}{1.5}\,(x - \bar{x})$, with $x = \mathrm{RH}_{50}/\V{si2_x30}{30}\,\%$,
so the truth lies inside B2's class; K checks the machinery, not power. Worlds K2a, K2b and K2c (realistic power) use
the same form with the slope at each temperature chosen so that the across-sorbent spread of the shift equals the
measured in-sample spread of $\log\kpp$ (\V{si2_sd27}{0.089} at \V{si2_T27}{27}~\textdegree C, slope
\V{si2_b27}{0.50}; \V{si2_sd60}{0.300} at \V{si2_T60}{60}~\textdegree C, slope \V{si2_b60}{1.38}), plus end-point
offsets drawn as in the null worlds: the largest effect of the measured size that the isotherm could carry, with
end-point error on top. In the six structure-free null worlds S0 to S5 the log rate constant of each sorbent and
temperature is shifted by a normal draw with the same spreads, independent of the isotherm, and the equilibrium is the
isotherm scaled by $(\C{1} + \varepsilon)$, with $|\varepsilon|$ resampled from the \V{si2_ngroups}{18} measured group
offsets, a random sign and $|\varepsilon\, w_{30}|$ capped at \V{si2_cap}{3.128}~wt\%; nothing any model sees predicts
these draws, so any beat is a false positive. World C3 (form error, reported only) is world C3 of Note~\snote{1}, a
rate law shared by every material but not of B0's form.

The harness passes if (\C{1}) no fold is beaten in N; (\C{2}) at least \V{si2_need}{7} of \V{si2_nfold2}{8} folds are
beaten in K and the median over folds and strata of $|b_{\mathrm{fit}}/\V{si2_bK2}{1.5} - \C{1}|$ is at most \V{si2_btol}{0.25}; and
(\C{3}) over the \V{si2_nnull}{48} null fold-fits, the one-sided \V{si2_cp}{95}\,\% Clopper--Pearson upper bound on the
per-fold beat rate is at most \V{si2_cpmax}{0.40}, no world has \V{si2_wmax}{5} or more of its folds beaten, and the
median of $r$ is below \V{si2_rmax}{2.5}\,\%. The bound of \V{si2_cpmax2}{0.40} keeps the probability of at least
\V{si2_need2}{7} beaten folds under the null at or below \V{si2_p7}{0.05} for a within-world fold correlation up to
\V{si2_rho}{0.12}; the observed within-world correlation of null beats is reported. If the harness fails, H4a$'$ is
not tested. The K2 and C3 results are reported whatever happens; if H4a$'$ is falsified, the K2 worlds set its
wording.

\paragraph{Result.} The harness passed all three criteria (Table~\ref{tab:harnessb2}). No fold was beaten in N. In K
all \V{si2_Kbeats}{8} folds were beaten and the fitted slope was recovered to within \V{si2_Kerr}{0.05}\,\% (median
over folds and strata). Of the \V{si2_nnull2}{48} null fold-fits, \V{si2_Sbeats}{3} were beaten, at most
\V{si2_Smax}{2} in any world; the upper bound on the per-fold beat rate was \V{si2_cpobs}{0.154}, the median of $r$
over the null folds \V{si2_rmed}{-2.7}\,\%, and the within-world correlation of null beats \V{si2_rhoobs}{0.064}, inside
the value the bound assumes. B2 was supported in two of the three realistic-power worlds (K2a and K2b,
\V{si2_K2a}{7} and \V{si2_K2b}{7} folds) and not established in K2c (\V{si2_K2c}{5} folds), so an effect of the
measured size is not always detected; a falsification on the real data is worded accordingly. In the form-error world
C3, B2 beat B0 in \V{si2_C3}{6} of the folds, with median $r$ of \V{si2_C3r}{52}\,\% and a negative fitted slope at both
temperatures: a rate law shared by every material but not of B0's form is enough to make the isotherm predict B0's
effective rate constant.

\section*{Supporting Note~\snote{4}: Strata, secondary metrics, exploratory checks}

The protocol reports the temperature and direction strata separately and names two secondary metrics: the relative
error in the time to \V{si4_t63}{63}\,\% of a segment's change, and the error truncated at five times the measured
time to \V{si4_t63b}{63}\,\%, so that long equilibrium tails do not dilute the transient (Table~\ref{tab:secondary}).
They were computed after the confirmatory runs with the parameters stored in them (no refit of B0 or B2), and the
recomputed predictions reproduce the confirmatory errors exactly. B2 improves on B0 in none of them, except by
\V{si4_st}{0.3}\,\% in one stratum under R1.

Two further checks were added after the result and are exploratory. First, a joint oracle applies both oracles at once:
B0 with the held-out sorbent's own end points and its own rate constant fitted on those scaled isotherms. It lowered
B0's median error by \V{si4_j2}{26}\,\% (R2) and \V{si4_j1}{25}\,\% (R1) at the median over held-out sorbents, about
the sum of the two separate effects. Second, Figure~\ref{fig:s1} plots each sorbent's own rate constant, fitted in
sample before the freeze, against its half-loading humidity; no stratum shows a significant rank correlation, though
with eight sorbents only strong correlations could be detected.
